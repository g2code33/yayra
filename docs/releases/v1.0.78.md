# Yayra v1.0.78 — full-card Documents opening

Released: 2026-10-22

## Documents hub

- Every available file card now exposes its entire surface as the primary open
  action. Supported PPTX, PDF, Word, spreadsheet, image, and text files open
  directly inside Yayra from the icon, filename, metadata, status, or empty
  card space.
- Unsupported formats continue to use their clearly labelled desktop-app route.
- **Desktop app**, **Show**, **Remove**, and **Locate…** remain independent
  actions and are never swallowed by the full-card target.
- The card action is keyboard accessible, has a visible focus state, announces
  its destination to assistive technology, and suppresses accidental duplicate
  opens from rapid clicks.
- Unavailable entries expose **Locate…** instead of a misleading open target.
